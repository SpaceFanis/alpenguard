# AlpenGuard demo site: brief

**Job and audience.** Ski tourers planning tomorrow's tour the evening before (Persuade mode, with one Operate fragment: the demo). They should leave believing "one map, with sources, and someone watching my return time" is worth trying. The primary action is **Try it on a sample tour** (jumps to the live demo).

**Outcome and proof.** The proof is the product working: a 3D map of a real Alpine tour, the critical point at km 3.8 computed from real elevation data, the bulletin and a French report pinned to it with their ages, then the overdue timer and dossier. There are no testimonials or metrics. The only stat is the 28 deaths in France by 24 Feb 2026 (UIAA / Petzl).

**Direction.** "The product performs" (taste draw, seed 1412472385). Dark pre-dawn ground, snow-white ink, EAWS orange as the single accent, and one face (Hanken Grotesk). The hero is the live map with the headline upper-left. Then:
1. intro column: the night-before problem
2. one trip card in four fields: planned → analysed → on the mountain → closed or dossier sent
3. the "I'm back" switch, blown up: flipping it swaps the field between calm and alert
4. the checks as a command list
5. AI reads, rules decide: facts + paragraph + sources/outputs
6. comparison rows
7. limits as a question index
8. tile footer with the team

The focal moment is the hero map flying to km 3.8.

**Scope.** A single page plus the working demo: load the sample route or upload a GPX, type or speak the plan, run the analysis, see the flags with sources, start the tour, fast-forward the clock, see the SMS/email dossier, and press "I'm back" to delete the data. The AI output is cached and labelled as such. Nothing is sent. Desktop and phone.

**Anti-goals.** Never call a slope "safe". No invented users or quotes. No purple, gradients, glass or icon cards. The alert never looks like it goes to 112.

**States.** No route loaded / analysing (worker progress) / flags found / no flags in the bulletin's aspects (and even then it does not say "safe") / tiles failed to load (a fallback message, and the engine still runs on the cached profile) / timer: waiting → overdue → dossier sent / checked in → data deleted. Reduced motion: no camera flights, instant state changes.
